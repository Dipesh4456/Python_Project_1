=== SKYNOVA ===

The world's cities are running out of power. The old energy grid has
failed, and only clean energy can restart it.

Five clean energy cores - Helios (sun), Aqua (water), Flora (plants),
Terra (earth) and Zephyr (wind) - are hidden in different cities of
the sky. Their places change every time you play!

Choose your starting city, fly between the cities and collect all five
cores. But beware: air shooters, lightning and tornadoes damage your
ship, heavy cargo burns more fuel, and only a few cities have a
station where you can refuel and repair. The stations move too!

Collect all five cores and the world's power is restored.
Lose all your health, or run out of fuel away from a station, and you lose.

This game is about Sustainable Development Goal :
Affordable and Clean Energy for everyone.
