HOW TO PLAY
-----------
move      Fly to a different sector (you will be asked for its name)
collect   Collect the crystal in your current sector (if there is one)
status    Show your current sector and the crystals in your cargo hold
save      Save your progress
help      Show these instructions again
lopeta    Save and quit the game

Your progress is saved under your pilot name. Next time you start
the game, enter the same name to continue where you left off.
