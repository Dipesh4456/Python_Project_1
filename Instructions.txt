HOW TO PLAY
-----------
GOAL: Collect all 5 clean energy cores hidden in the 10 cities:
Helios, Aqua, Flora, Terra and Zephyr.
The cores and the fuel stations are in different cities.

YOU LOSE if your ship health reaches 0, or if you try to fly
without enough fuel when you are not in a station city.

COMMANDS
travel    Fly to a connected city (uses fuel, may cause danger)
          Shortcut: just type the city name, e.g. 'Wind Plains',
          or 'travel Wind Plains'
collect   Collect the core in your current city
status    Show health, fuel, cores and cargo weight
look      Show the city and its flight routes
refuel    Fill your tank (only in cities with a station)
repair    Repair your ship, +30 health (only in cities with a station)
save      Save your progress
help      Show these instructions again
lopeta    Save and quit the game

TIPS
- Only 4 cities are marked [STATION].
  Plan your trip around them.
- Heavy cargo makes every flight cost more fuel.
- Dangerous routes (HIGH) are risky: air shooters, lightning, tornadoes.
- Enter the same name next time to continue a saved game.
